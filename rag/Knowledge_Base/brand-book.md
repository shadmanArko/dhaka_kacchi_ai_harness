# Dhaka Kacchi Berlin — Brand Book

## 1. Who we are

**Dhaka Kacchi Berlin** is a small, online kitchen in Berlin. We cook one dish the way Old Dhaka cooks it
for weddings and celebrations: **kacchi biryani**, together with its traditional companion, **Borhani**.

**One line:** *The real Old Dhaka kacchi, cooked fresh every Saturday in Berlin.*

## 2. Our story

We were founded in spring 2026 by a Bangladeshi doctor who came to Berlin to get her German medical licence. Between
her studies, her young family and life in a new country, she missed one thing above all: real kacchi from Old Dhaka.
She searched Berlin and could not find it.

One evening she invited friends home and cooked her smoky Dhaka-style kacchi. Everyone said the same thing: *"We've
never had kacchi like this in Germany. Open a place so we can buy it!"* From that one dinner, Dhaka Kacchi began.

Our first public day was at a community event (Meena Bazar, 1 May 2026). We arrived late, and everything sold out
within an hour. Since then we have cooked every Saturday, and we have grown from a 10-order minimum to 20–25 slots a
week. We have served Bangladeshi, Indian, Pakistani and German guests, and people who travelled from Hamburg and
Munich just to collect a box.

**What the story means:** we are not a restaurant chain. We are a family kitchen that takes time, refuses
shortcuts, and cooks for people who miss home, and for people who have never tasted home-style Bangladeshi food
before.

**How we cook it:**
1. Fresh halal mutton, marinated overnight (about 24 hours) in yoghurt, raw papaya and our spice blend.
2. More than 45 spices, roasted and hand-blended in-house before every batch. No packets, no ready-made masala.
3. Premium basmati, part-cooked and layered with the raw meat, potatoes, ghee, saffron and beresta.
4. The pot is sealed and cooked on dum from 5 a.m. on Saturday for 6+ hours. We don't open the lid.
5. One fresh batch per week, packed for the same day. Never reheated. [CONFIRM: "never frozen"? The website says so.]

## 6. Frequently asked questions

**What is kacchi biryani?**
"Kacchi" means raw. The meat goes into the pot raw and marinated, layered with rice, and both cook together under a
sealed lid (dum). Most biryani cooks the meat first; kacchi cooks it together with the rice, so every grain takes on
the flavour of the meat and spices. It is the celebration dish of Dhaka.

**What is the potato doing in my biryani?**
The dum potato is a Dhaka signature. It slowly absorbs the ghee, saffron and meat juices. Many people say it's the
best bite in the box.
